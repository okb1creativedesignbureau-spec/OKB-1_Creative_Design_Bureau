# Aircraft Projects

[Aircraft Projects](https://okb1creativedesignbureau-spec.github.io/OKB-1_Creative_Design_Bureau/aircraft-index.html)

This section compiles all aviation-related work produced over the last several years, ranging from historical CAD restorations to modern aerodynamic analysis. The purpose of these projects is not only to recreate legacy airframes but also to explore engineering decisions, structural layouts, and aerodynamic performance characteristics through modern digital tools.

Most models presented here originate from limited archival material such as brochures, partial drawings, engineering sketches, or museum references. Each aircraft is rebuilt with accurate proportions and geometry, followed by surface checks and manufacturing-style cleanup. Some projects include CFD studies, stability observations, or structural approximations, depending on the available data.

These aircraft reconstructions represent a mixture of educational research, engineering curiosity, and digital preservation work. Many of the aircraft featured here never entered full production, making the CAD models useful for historical reference or conceptual aerodynamic study.

## Featured Articles
- **IPTN N-2130 Reconstruction** — A complete 3D rebirth of Indonesia’s cancelled 100–130 seat jetliner, built from brochure data and engineering references.
- **PTDI R-2130 Reconstruction** — A what-if 3D rebirth of Indonesia’s cancelled continuation of the N-2130 jetliner, built with super critical winglet, revised nose aerodynamics and revised vertical stabilizer contour data from engineering references.
- **Dassault Mercure CAD** — A high-fidelity surface model of the rare Dassault Mercure, including modernized skin work and render experiments.

# Engineering & Technical Models

[Engineering and Technical Models Main Site](https://okb1creativedesignbureau-spec.github.io/OKB-1_Creative_Design_Bureau/Engineering-and-Technical-Models.html)

This section contains mechanical designs, prototype studies, CFD setups, and various technical models. Many of these works originate from academic projects, independent engineering experiments, or tool design for practical field work.

The focus is on real mechanical behavior, manufacturable geometry, and analysis-ready surfaces. Some items here were created to validate physical assumptions through simulation, such as flow behavior, mechanical interaction, or structural feasibility. Others are utility tools and engineering constructions that were later turned into shareable 3D models.

Over time, this repository has accumulated experimental turbine designs, precision layout tools, structural components, and general-purpose mechanical assemblies. Several models include real measurement standards and can be exported for 3D printing or further modification.

# Research & Analysis

[Research and Analysis Main Page](https://okb1creativedesignbureau-spec.github.io/OKB-1_Creative_Design_Bureau/Research-and-Analysis.html)

This section houses technical investigations, datasets, measurement logs, simulations, and analytical studies. Many of these works derive from field data collection, physics exploration, environmental measurements, or academic assignments reworked into clearer, accessible formats.

Each entry aims to provide reproducible information: detailed tables, clear methodology explanations, and computation steps when relevant. Topics range from atmospheric studies and lapse rate calculations to thermodynamic observations and applied physics. Because much of this work was originally created for academic or personal research purposes, the content here retains a scientific tone and methodological clarity.

These research notes also function as reference material for engineering or CFD projects elsewhere on the site, providing necessary inputs such as boundary conditions, measured parameters, or environmental assumptions.

# Miscellaneous Projects

This section collects smaller works, creative models, experimental concepts, and various CAD files that do not fit strictly into aircraft, engineering, or research categories. Many of these items were created spontaneously—from Thingiverse-style hobby models to abandoned prototypes or early drafts of larger engineering projects.
