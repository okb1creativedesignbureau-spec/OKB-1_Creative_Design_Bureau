# About

Hi! I’m an experimentator, and this website is where I share my engineering projects, 3D models, and experimental builds.
Most of the work here comes from years of university projects, CAD explorations, CFD experiments, and various side-designs that never made it into production—but still deserve a place on the internet.

I publish my work openly so others can learn from it, remix it, or use it as reference material. Whether it’s aircraft CAD restorations, mechanical components, CFD notes, or random engineering leftovers, I try to document everything as clearly and simply as possible.

If you’re interested in 3D modeling, simulation, or engineering design, feel free to explore around. More uploads will be added gradually (as long as my archive of old projects doesn’t run out!).
