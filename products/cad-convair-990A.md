# Convair 990A “Coronado”

Advanced Production Variant – High-Speed Jetliner

## Overview

The Convair 990A Coronado represents the final and most refined production form of Convair’s high-speed jetliner concept. While the original 990 was designed as an aerodynamic leap beyond the Convair 880, the 990A reflects what happens when an ambitious design meets real-world airline feedback, operational constraints, and iterative engineering fixes. This model represents the matured 990 platform, incorporating pylon, nacelle, and thrust-reverser changes that define the 990A and later Astrojet-style aircraft.

## What Makes the 990A Different

Compared to the baseline Convair 990, the 990A incorporates multiple real-world refinements:

- Revised engine pylons to reduce drag and structural issues
- Modified nacelle and thrust-reverser geometry
- Improved high-speed and cruise efficiency
- Better compatibility with airline operating practices

These changes were not cosmetic — they were part of Convair’s attempt to stabilize, streamline, and modernize the 990 into a viable production jetliner.

## Reverse-Thrust & Nacelle Variants

This project reflects the two historically important 990A hardware philosophies:

**European-Style (Swissair / Internord)**

This version represents the early European interpretation of the Convair 990A — a design that prioritized mechanical clarity and direct functionality over aerodynamic streamlining. Its defining feature is the distinctive “shell-clamp” thrust reverser, with curved, petal-like doors and exposed mechanical structure that gives the nacelles a very industrial, jet-age character. 

This configuration feels:

- Raw
- Mechanical
- Purpose-built

It reflects how European operators adopted the 990A as a high-speed precision machine, emphasizing controllability and operational transparency over ultimate drag reduction. Think of it as the engineering-first 990A.

**American-Style (Later Series / Astrojet Direction)**

This version reflects Convair’s push toward a more aerodynamically refined and commercially polished 990A. The nacelles gain extended spats and a tapered conical tail fairing, smoothing airflow and giving the engines a distinctly more “jet age futurism” appearance.

This configuration feels:

- Sleeker
- Faster
- More production-ready

It represents the moment when the 990A stopped being a testbed and started trying to become a true Astrojet — optimized for cruise efficiency, visual elegance, and airline branding. Think of it as the marketing-polished 990A.

## Design Philosophy

This model treats the 990A as: “What the Convair 990 became once reality forced it to grow up.”

The wing and anti-shock body system remains. The high-Mach cruise intent is preserved. But the engine installation is refined for reliability and airline use. It represents the practical peak of the Convair speedliner program.
