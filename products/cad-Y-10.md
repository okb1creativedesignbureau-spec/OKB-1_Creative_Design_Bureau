# Shanghai Y-10 — China’s First Jet That Almost Changed Everything

## What It Was  
China’s first domestically developed large jet airliner of the 1970s — bold, ambitious, and ahead of its ecosystem. A nation with limited resources built and flew a full-scale four-engine jet. It existed against the odds. It was driven by national ambition, self-reliance, and symbolic technological independence. By the time it flew, the world had already moved on. The program was cancelled before it could mature.

## Why It Was Forgotten  
No mass production. No airline service. No continuation — just two prototypes and silence. To digitally preserve a lost chapter of aerospace history — and let it fly again in CAD.
