# Free Community Releases 

A portion of the work in this section is distributed freely on **Thingiverse**, where I share print-ready models for the wider maker and engineering community. These uploads include small mechanical tools, aerodynamic test shapes, simple fixtures, and creative concept designs that do not require the full depth of engineering documentation found elsewhere on this site.

The goal of these free releases is to provide learning resources for students and hobbyists, while also contributing to the open-source 3D printing ecosystem. Many of these models come from experimental drafts or early prototypes that were later refined into larger projects.

All models on Thingiverse are available at no cost and can be downloaded, printed, modified, or remixed under the permissive licenses provided.

## 30 cm → 8 cm Funnel Adapter for IKEA IDEALISK to small Jar >> Version 1.0

30 cm → 8 cm Funnel Adapter for IKEA IDEALISK + Lion Star Jar 

Need to channel a full-size 30 cm IKEA IDEALISK colander (inner side) into a compact 8 cm container?
This adapter funnel is designed exactly for that — turning a wide Swedish kitchen tool into a perfectly aimed Indonesian storage solution… printed on a Chinese Elegoo Saturn 6 Max. A true global collaboration.

**Why I Designed This**

I wanted to transfer ingredients from large batches directly into a Lion Star jar, which has an ~8 cm opening. Most funnels are too small or too narrow for the flow rate needed, and the IDEALISK colander already does a great job at holding/straining material — it just needed a proper “neck.”

So this part acts as a precision-fit funnel adapter.

Features 30 cm inner-diameter top flange matches the inside of the IKEA IDEALISK colander. 8 cm outer-diameter bottom spout for Lion Star jars or similar containers. Smooth inner walls for faster flow. Strong, thick walls for durability (especially with PLA). Works with powders, grains, nuts, dried foods, or general workshop materials. Print Settings Material: PLA (recommended), PETG or ABS also fine Layer Height: 0.2 mm Grid Infill: 15–20% is enough Supports: No Orientation: Print upright, spout pointing down Printer Used: Elegoo Saturn 4 Max (model works for FDM) 

Assembly / Use 
Just place the adapter inside the IKEA IDEALISK colander and set the spout into your Lion Star jar. The colander stays stable thanks to its wide contact area.
Compatibility IKEA IDEALISK 30 cm metal colander (inner lip) Lion Star jars (≈8 cm opening) Any other container with an outer diameter of ~8 cm

## 30 cm → 8 cm Funnel Adapter for IKEA IDEALISK to small Jar >> Version 2.0

30 cm → 8 cm Funnel Adapter for IKEA IDEALISK + Lion Star Jar 

Need to channel a full-size 30 cm IKEA IDEALISK colander (inner side) into a compact 8 cm container?
This adapter funnel is designed exactly for that — turning a wide Swedish kitchen tool into a perfectly aimed Indonesian storage solution… printed on a Chinese Elegoo Saturn 6 Max. A true global collaboration.

**Why I Designed This**

I wanted to transfer ingredients from large batches directly into a Lion Star jar, which has an ~8 cm opening. The V2.0 now featured a exponential curved for better flow and laminarity which I confirmed via CFD tools such as Ansys. Yes, I am that nerd. Thank you. Lol

Same as the V1.0, the V2.0 printability have same setting. Features 30 cm inner-diameter top flange matches the inside of the IKEA IDEALISK colander. 8 cm outer-diameter bottom spout for Lion Star jars or similar containers. Smooth inner walls for faster flow. Strong, thick walls for durability (especially with PLA). Works with powders, grains, nuts, dried foods, or general workshop materials. Print Settings Material: PLA (recommended), PETG or ABS also fine Layer Height: 0.2 mm Honeycomb Infill: 5–10% is enough Supports: No Orientation: Print upright, spout pointing down Printer Used: Elegoo Saturn 4 Max again as the V1.0

Assembly / Use 
Just place the adapter inside the IKEA IDEALISK colander and set the spout into your Lion Star jar. The colander stays stable thanks to its wide contact area. This V2.0 also proven to have better compression strength compared to the V1.0 due to the characteristic of the monocoque-rigid cody behavior. This logics comes to the fact that an edged body statically have greater shear factor in the meet up points due to different force factor than the gradual curve of the V2.0 version

## Brewing Kit Storage Tube (Dual Hydrometer + Thermometer Stick + Drying Rods) 

Brewing Kit Storage Tube (Dual Hydrometer + Thermometer Stick + Drying Rods) 
This cylindrical storage tube is designed specifically for homebrewers who want their measurement tools safely organized and protected.

The container holds:
- 1 × finished-alcohol hydrometer 1 × OG/RG hydrometer 1 × stick for securely holding a thermometer probe 1 × stick for drying hydrometer test tubes 
- The internal compartments prevent glass instruments from shifting or hitting each other, reducing risk of damage. The tube protects against dust, humidity, and accidental impact.
- Whether you brew beer, kombucha, mead, or spirits, this kit keeps your instruments neatly stored in one place, making your brewing workflow faster and more organized.

This design have a direct root from my experience as a homebrew hobbyiest, which I brew many of my own beverages for the Food Science Educational class purposes.
